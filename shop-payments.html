Payment Information

Store Registration Payment

Your ₦20,000 basic store fee becomes payable only after your store application is approved. You should not pay before approval.

Premium Customisation

The ₦10,000 premium customisation upgrade is optional. It is separate from the basic store registration fee.

Promotion Payments

Promotion requests are not automatically paid or activated. A promotion becomes active only after the required payment and approval process.

Customer Checkout Charges

EarnGig applies a checkout service charge to customer purchases. The charge is between ₦100 and ₦1,000, depending on purchase value. The final calculation is not displayed here yet.

Payment security: This page is a frontend record viewer. It does not collect money, verify bank transfers or process Paystack transactions. Only verified payment records should be treated as successful payments.
