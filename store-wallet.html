How Your Store Wallet Works

Store sales are paid in Naira

Money from successful customer purchases is credited to your Seller Store Wallet in Nigerian Naira.

Separate from game rewards

Your store money cannot be used as gaming coins and does not enter your normal EarnGig rewards wallet.

Pending settlements

Payments that have not completed the settlement process remain pending and cannot be withdrawn.

Withdraw to your bank

When funds become available, you can request a bank withdrawal through the separate withdrawals page.
