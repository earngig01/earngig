How Your Seller Wallet Works

1. Buyer pays

After successful checkout, the seller's product proceeds are held as pending settlement.

2. You dispatch

Accept the order, prepare the package and upload a package photo before dispatch.

3. Both confirm

The buyer confirms receipt and you confirm delivery. Each vendor order is settled separately.

4. Funds become available

After both confirmations, the sale becomes eligible for your available Naira Store Wallet balance.
