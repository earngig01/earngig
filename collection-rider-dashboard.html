Collection rider workflow

1. Accept vendor orders and collect them before the end of the same calendar day.

2. Upload parcel pickup proof and obtain vendor handover confirmation.

3. Group collected parcels by destination state and town.

4. Select an approved storekeeper and prepare a bulk shipment.

5. Arrange transport, upload dispatch proof and record transport costs.

6. Wait for the storekeeper to physically confirm receipt and parcel count.

7. Your agreed shipment earnings become payable after verified receipt.
