Choose how you want to work

EarnGig has three delivery roles. Choose the role that matches the service you want to provide.

🏍️

Delivery Rider

Collect orders from vendors and deliver them directly to customers. Work within your registered service area and earn for every successfully completed delivery.

📦

Collection Rider

Collect multiple orders from vendors, organize parcels by destination and arrange consolidated shipments to registered storekeepers in other locations.

🏪

Storekeeper

Receive interstate shipments, organize individual customer parcels, keep them securely and release each parcel after verifying the customer's private pickup code.

Separate earnings: Delivery riders, collection riders and storekeepers have separate Delivery Wallets. Each role earns according to its verified delivery or handover responsibilities. Delivery wallet withdrawals carry a 2% service charge.

How EarnGig Delivery works

Every order is tracked separately, and responsibility transfers to the next participant after a verified handover.

1

Vendor prepares order

Vendor confirms the order, packages it and uploads a photograph before releasing it for collection.

2

Rider collects

An eligible rider accepts the pickup, visits the vendor that same day and uploads collection evidence.

3

Delivery or shipment

A normal rider delivers directly. A collection rider groups interstate orders and sends them to their assigned storekeepers.

4

Customer confirms

Customer gives the private confirmation code. The responsible participant verifies the handover and earns the applicable fee.

Important delivery rules

✓ Once a rider accepts a pickup, collection must take place that same calendar day.
✓ Every order must be completed within a maximum of five calendar days from its original release.
✓ Riders can accept nearby orders from different vendors. Each vendor order remains separately tracked.
✓ Photographic evidence is required at the applicable collection and dispatch stages.
✓ Storekeepers must verify the customer's private pickup code before releasing a parcel.
✓ Delivery earnings are credited to the appropriate separate Delivery Wallet after verified completion.
✓ Delivery participants must not demand extra unapproved payments from customers.
✓ All delivery wallet withdrawals carry a 2% service charge.
