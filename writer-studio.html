How Reader Completion Works

Each published novel has a reader quantity selected by its writer. The progress tracker shows how many readers have successfully completed the novel compared with the selected quantity. A reader only counts after successfully completing the reading process and passing the novel's comprehension questions. Writers earn 250 coins for each qualifying reader.
