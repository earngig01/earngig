How the Writer Programme Works

1. Create your novel and write all its chapters.
2. Save your work whenever you want.
3. Submit your completed novel for admin screening.
4. If approved, choose your reader quantity and pay the applicable upload fee through Paystack.
5. Once payment is verified, your novel can be published.
6. Earn 250 coins for every reader who successfully completes your novel.
