How vendor handover works

  1. Receive a customer order and prepare the parcel.
  2. Confirm the products and package the order.
  3. Upload a clear photograph of the packed parcel.
  4. Wait for a registered rider to accept the delivery.
  5. When the rider arrives, check their identity and order reference.
  6. The rider uploads pickup proof.
  7. Physically hand the parcel to the rider.
  8. Confirm the physical handover on this dashboard.
  9. Keep the handover confirmation for your records.
A parcel photograph alone does not confirm a handover or release payment. The platform must verify the actual transfer of responsibility.
