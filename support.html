Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an EarnGig account?
Visit the registration page, enter the required information and follow the registration instructions to create your account.
What should I do if I forget my password?
Use the password recovery option on the login page if available. If you still cannot access your account, contact support.
How do I earn coins?
EarnGig offers different earning activities. These may include eligible games, reading novels, referrals and other approved activities. Each activity has its own reward conditions.
Why have my coins not been credited?
Some activities require successful completion or verification before rewards are credited. If your reward is still missing, send support the activity name and approximate completion time.
How do I withdraw my earnings?
Check your wallet for the available withdrawal options, minimum withdrawal requirements and instructions. Only submit withdrawal requests through official EarnGig pages.
What should I do if my withdrawal is delayed?
Check the transaction status in your wallet. If the transaction remains unresolved, contact support with your transaction reference. Never share your password or PIN.
How does the referral programme work?
Share your referral link with others. Referral rewards are subject to the programme's eligibility requirements and successful referral verification.
Why did I not receive a game reward?
Rewards may depend on completing the required game objective. Check the instructions for that game and contact support if you believe your completed activity was not recognised.
Do I earn coins just by opening a novel?
No. Opening a novel does not earn a reward. Readers must complete the novel and pass its story-specific comprehension questions to qualify for the reading reward.
How can I become an EarnGig writer?
Visit the Become a Writer page and submit your application. Approved writers can create novels and submit completed work for admin screening.
When do writers pay the novel upload fee?
Writers pay only after their submitted novel has been approved. The upload fee depends on the selected reader target and is paid in naira, not coins.
How do I buy something on the marketplace?
Browse available listings, review the product information and follow the purchase instructions displayed on the listing.
What if an EarnGig page is not loading?
Check your internet connection, refresh the page and try again. You can also try another browser or clear your browser cache.
