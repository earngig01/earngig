Important pricing notes:

  • Where collection service is required, the collection rider fee is ₦1,000 per order.
  • Same-state storekeeper fees must not exceed ₦1,000 per order.
  • Abuja-to-other-zone rates use the separate Abuja zone allocation above.
  • The original chart does not specify rates for other states sending to Abuja. Those routes are marked as awaiting an approved rate rather than being assigned an invented price.
  • Transport-driver charges and collection rider earnings must be handled separately from the storekeeper fee.
