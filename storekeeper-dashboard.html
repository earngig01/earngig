How your collection point works

1. A collection rider sends a bulk shipment to your registered collection point.

2. You physically inspect the shipment and confirm the number of parcels received.

3. The bulk shipment is separated into individual customer parcels.

4. Contact customers to inform them their parcels are ready.

5. Verify each customer's private pickup code when they arrive.

6. Your approved storekeeper fee is credited for each successfully verified collection.
