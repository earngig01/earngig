How to play

1. Choose your demo coin stake.
2. Look at the displayed card.
3. Select Higher or Lower.
4. If your prediction is correct, the demo credits the stated total prize of 3× your stake.
5. If your prediction is wrong, the demo stake is lost.
6. An equal-value card is a draw and the demo stake is returned.
