When Can I Withdraw?

Mining rewards are accumulated in your Mining Wallet. You can transfer them to your main wallet when the transfer service is enabled.

Normal G-Coin wallet withdrawal requests are accepted on the 27th and 28th of each month. The applicable withdrawal fee will be deducted according to EarnGig's withdrawal rules.
