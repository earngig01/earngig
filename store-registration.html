Register Your Store

Want to sell on EarnGig? Submit your business information and verification documents. Our administration team will review your application before you can pay the shop opening fee and create your store.

1
Complete Application Provide your personal and business information.
2
Admin Verification EarnGig reviews your details and evidence.
3
Pay Shop Fee Approved applicants pay ₦20,000.
4
Set Up Your Shop Complete your storefront and list products.
Important: Submitting this application does not automatically open a store. EarnGig will review your information first. You will only be asked to pay the ₦20,000 shop opening fee after your application is approved. Please submit genuine and accurate information.
SECTION 01

Personal Information

Tell us who is applying to operate this store.

SECTION 02

Identity Verification

Provide identity information and a clear photograph of yourself.

Only provide the document type and requested verification evidence through the secure upload system.
🪪
Upload a clear passport photograph

Your face should be clearly visible. Do not upload an edited or misleading image.

📄
Upload your identity document

Upload a clear image of the selected document. All relevant details should be readable.

SECTION 03

Business Information

Provide details about the business you intend to operate on EarnGig.

SECTION 04

Business Documents and Evidence

Help EarnGig establish that your business is genuine and that you are authorised to operate it.

📑
Upload CAC or other registration evidence

If your business is registered, upload the relevant certificate or document. If not, select the declaration below.

📍
Upload evidence of your business location

Examples include a business premises photograph, shop sign, relevant utility document or tenancy evidence. Avoid exposing unrelated sensitive information.

SECTION 05

Physical Product Verification

Show the actual products you intend to sell. These photographs help our team verify that you have genuine products in your possession or control.

📦
Upload at least two product photographs

Photograph the actual products you handle. Do not use downloaded catalogue images as proof of possession.

🤳
Upload a photograph of yourself holding one of your products

Your face and the product should both be clearly visible in the same photograph. The photograph must be genuine and recent.

SECTION 06

Additional Information

Provide any other information that will help our verification team assess your application.

SECTION 07

Shop Opening Payment

Payment comes after admin approval, not at the application stage.

Basic EarnGig Shop

₦20,000

One-time shop opening payment, payable only after your store registration has been approved.

  • Basic storefront setup
  • Business information and branding
  • Product listing access
  • Seller dashboard
  • Order management access
Do not make any payment now. If your application is approved, your Seller Dashboard will provide the next payment step. Premium customisation is an optional ₦10,000 upgrade after opening your shop.

Applicant Declaration

Confirm that the information you have supplied is accurate.
