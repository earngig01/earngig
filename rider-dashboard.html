Important delivery rules

Pickup deadline: After accepting a vendor order, collect it before the end of that same calendar day.

Vendor handover: Upload pickup proof and wait for the vendor to confirm physical handover before proceeding.

Delivery deadline: Every order must be completed within five calendar days from its original release date. Handovers do not reset this deadline.

Customer confirmation: A delivery is completed only after the customer's private confirmation code is verified.
